# Six new songs — drafts (2026-07-10)

Lyrics drafted in the register of the existing three (`data/songs.js`): short
declarative lines, loose neo-Sindarin, per-line English. Deliberate
cross-references to the existing set are noted per song. Once a track is
composed and mastered, move its lyrics into `data/songs.js`, drop the audio in
`audio/`, then run `build-lyrics-txt.mjs` + `transcribe-tengwar.mjs`.

**Suno pronunciation tips** (for the lyrics box, not the canonical text):

- Sindarin `c` is always /k/ — consider spelling `celeb → keleb`, `cuil → kuil`
  in the pasted lyrics so Suno doesn't soften it.
- `dh` = voiced th ("this"); if it comes out wrong, try spelling it `th`.
- Circumflexes/accents are fine; Suno reads through them.
- Tag sections `[Verse]` / `[Chorus]` / `[Bridge]` / `[Outro]` as usual.

## 1. Tôl Aur — "Day Shall Come" (Ember)

pairs with **I Dann Haer** ("níniel ornoth" = tears without count) and reuses
"odog" from **I Dhae Odog** and "Cennin…" from **Gwannad i-Lû**.

**Suno style prompt:**
> Dark Chamber Folk, Martial Percussion, Solo Cello, Deep Contralto Vocals,
> Low Male Chant, Minor Key, Mid-Slow Tempo, Grim, Defiant, War Lament,
> Minimalist, Reverb

## 3. I Ngelaidh — "The Two Trees" (all three themes)

Laurelin and Telperion; gold verse, silver verse, ember ending. The site's
three themes *are* the Trees — the stage could switch page theme per section.
"Thinnar" echoes **I Dann Haer**; the drinking motif echoes "Sogon o-eithel
Anor" in **Gwannad i-Lû**, inverted: there you drink the light, here Ungoliant
does.

**Suno style prompt:**
> Dark Neoclassical Requiem, Funeral Folk, Small Choir, Organ Drone, Solo
> Cello, Deep Contralto Vocals, Minor Key, Very Slow, Mournful, Ends A

## 5. I Lind Iaur — "The Ancient Song" (Twilight Gold)

The Ainulindalë: the Music that made the world still echoing in water; the
discord woven in, not erased; the promise of the Second Music. Meta-fit —
*lind* is the domain. "Rista" echoes "Risto Menel!" from **I Dhae Odog**;
"ú-fîr" answers "dan ú-firin" from **Gwannad i-Lû**.

**Suno style prompt:**
> Dark Choral Drone, Neoclassical, Layered Voices, Dissonant Middle Section,
> Solo Cello, No Drums, Very Slow, Vast, Cold, Timeless, Mournful, Minimalist

**Structure:** verse / verse / chorus / bridge / chorus / outro

### Verse 1
| Sindarin | English |
|---|---|
| Nu-il nad, i-Lind. | Before all things, the Song. |
| Linnar i-lammath bain. | All the voices sing. |
| Uin-lind, onnen i-ardhon. | From the song, the world was born. |
| A mi-il nen, dartha. | And in every water, it remains. |

### Verse 2 *(the discord)*
| Sindarin | English |
|---|---|

| Rista i-glîr. | It rends the song. |

### Chorus

## 6. Athar i-Gaear — "Beyond the Sea" (Ember → Silver)

Drowned Beleriand, the world bent round, the Straight Road. Distinct from
**Gwannad i-Lû** (weariness of time): this one is about what lies *under* the
sea versus what lies *beyond* it. "I-thaim" echoes **I Dann Haer**'s greying
halls; the gulls are Legolas's sea-longing; "erui" closes the set's loneliest
thread.

**Suno style prompt:**
> Funeral Folk, Slow 6/8 Tide Rhythm, Solo Cello, Bowed Double Bass, Deep
> Contralto Vocals, Distant Waves And Gull Cries, Drone, Mournful, Resigned,
> Doom-Laden, Minimalist, Reverb
